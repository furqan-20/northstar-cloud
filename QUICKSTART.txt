NORTHSTAR — RUN ON YOUR LAPTOP
============================

1. Extract this ZIP. Open the northstar folder (the folder with server.py).
2. Open a terminal in that folder.
3. Run:

   python3 --version
   python3 server.py

4. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 in your browser.
5. Keep the terminal running. Ctrl+C stops the server.

Requires Python 3.10 or later. No pip or npm installation is needed.
On Windows use: py -3 server.py, or double-click start.bat.
On Linux/macOS you may also use: bash start.sh
If port 8000 is busy: python3 server.py --port 8001
Then use http://127.0.0.1:8001 instead.
